How far ahead should I book?

At least 7 days for party packs and 2–3 weeks for the on-site cart, especially on weekends in summer.

Do you deliver?

Yes, within a set radius of the shop. Delivery fees depend on distance and are confirmed with your quote.

How is the ice kept from melting?

Party packs arrive in insulated coolers with ice shaved fresh right before drop-off. Serve within about 90 minutes for the best texture.

Do I pay a deposit?

A 25% deposit locks your date. The balance is due the day before the event, paid online.
